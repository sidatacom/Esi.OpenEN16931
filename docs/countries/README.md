## Detailed Country Profiles

### 🇩🇪 Germany — XRechnung Leader, Factur-X Widespread
- **Mandatory since:** 2021 for all public-sector invoices
- **Primary standard:** XRechnung CIUS DE (Core Implementation of Uniform Standards)
- **Secondary adoption:** Factur-X / ZUGFeRD widely adopted in private sector (e.g., SAP Ariba, DATEV)
- **Note:** Germany is the most advanced country for both standards

### 🇫🇷 France — Factur-X Pioneer
- **Mandatory since:** 2023 for all business-to-business and public-sector invoices
- **Primary standard:** Factur-X (UBL + CII hybrid)
- **Secondary adoption:** UBL-based formats for international transactions
- **Note:** France has the most aggressive Factur-X adoption in Europe

### 🇮🇹 Italy — Factur-X Mandate
- **Mandatory since:** 2019 via national decree D.Lgs. 83/2019 (first EU country)
- **Primary standard:** Factur-X mandatory for all transactions
- **Secondary adoption:** XRechnung for public-sector transactions with German entities
- **Note:** Italy was the first EU country to mandate Factur-X nationally

### 🇳🇱 Netherlands — Dual Adoption, Testbed Nation
- **Mandatory since:** 2019 for public sector
- **Primary standards:** Both XRechnung and Factur-X mandatory for public sector
- **Secondary adoption:** PEPPOL BIS 3 widely used for cross-border EU transactions
- **Note:** Netherlands often acts as a testbed for new e-invoicing standards

### 🇪🇸 Spain — Strong Public Sector, XRechnung CIUS ES
- **Mandatory since:** 2018
- **Primary standard:** XRechnung CIUS ES (Spanish national profile)
- **Secondary adoption:** Factur-X gaining traction in retail and logistics
- **Note:** Spain's public procurement system is among the most advanced in Europe

### 🇦🇹 Austria — ebInterface + XRechnung
- **Mandatory since:** 2019 for public sector (ebInterface)
- **Primary standards:** UBL-based ebInterface and XRechnung
- **Secondary adoption:** Factur-X widely used in private sector
- **Note:** Austria uses the Austrian ebInterface standard alongside XRechnung

### 🇧🇪 Belgium — Comprehensive Adoption
- **Mandatory since:** 2019 for public sector
- **Primary standards:** UBL-based format and Factur-X

- **Note:** Belgium has comprehensive adoption across multiple formats

### 🇵🇱 Poland — Transitioning to Full Mandate

- **Secondary adoption:** XRechnung and Factur-X growing in private sector
- **Note:** Poland is transitioning from partial to full mandate coverage

### 🇸🇪 Sweden — Strong Private Sector Adoption
